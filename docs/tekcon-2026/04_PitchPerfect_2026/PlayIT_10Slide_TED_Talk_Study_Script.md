## Rapid Judge Q&A Defense Notes

1. **Did children actually improve their reading?**  
   "Our `N = 30` capstone study measured engagement (`15/16` happiest face), usability (`75.5` SUS), and teacher pedagogy (`4.73/5`). Measuring fluency gains directly is the goal of our proposed 6-week Phase 1 Cebu pilot."
2. **How do offline GCash / voucher payments prevent piracy without internet?**  
   "Signed offline activation codes bound to device ID and saved in Room SQLite—parents purchase via GCash or school voucher once, redeem offline, and never need recurring cloud streaming."
3. **Why is the APK ~114 MB, and can budget phones run it?**  
   "Because the Vosk speech model and phoneme audio ship inside the APK—so after one phone-to-phone Nearby Share transfer, it runs on Android 8.0+ with zero data charges ever again."
4. **Audio licensing before commercial launch?**  
   "Four clips (`AM`, `TUB`, `YAM`, `/i/`) used ElevenLabs Free Tier in the academic build and are credited in Parent Zone; all four swap to Apache-2.0 `Kokoro-82M` voices before the commercial pilot."
5. **What is built today vs commercial roadmap?**  
   "Built & tested today: all 4 steps across 7 chapters / 26 letters, offline Vosk ASR, mouth-shape cues, Room SQLite, Parent Zone A4 PDF export, zero `INTERNET` permission, `691` passing tests. Commercial roadmap: offline vouchers, multi-student Teacher Dashboard, and Bisaya/Tagalog packs."
