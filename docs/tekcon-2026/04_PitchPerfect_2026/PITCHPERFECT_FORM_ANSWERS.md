## 3. Project/Idea Details (Verified Word & Character Counts)

### Title of Project / Product Name\*
`PlayIT`

### Short description of the idea or project.\* *(23 / 30 words)*
PlayIT is an offline-first Android phonics app for Grade 1 Filipino learners pairing DepEd Marungko lessons with on-device speech recognition and gamified practice.

### Problem Statement\* *(127 / 200 words · 837 / 1,500 chars)*
According to the June 2022 World Bank and UNESCO Learning Poverty Brief, 90.9% (9 out of 10) of Filipino 10-year-olds still struggle to read a simple text. Grade 1 learners need patient, one-on-one phonics guidance connecting letter sounds with spoken words, yet overcrowded classrooms limit individual practice and working parents often lack the time or phonics training to coach at home.

Existing digital tools leave families behind two walls: the Connectivity Wall—cloud-based reading apps fail or stall on prepaid and patchy mobile data common in low-income households—and the Affordability Wall—subscriptions costing PHP 400 to PHP 600 per month or private tutors at PHP 3,000+ per month are out of reach, while foreign-accent voice models and unguided flashcard apps either cannot evaluate speech or mismatch DepEd phonics pacing.

### Target Market\* *(81 / 100 words · 542 / 600 chars)*
End users are Grade 1 Filipino learners (ages 6-7) building English phonics skills. Paying and enabling bridge customers include parents with budget Android 8.0+ phones (B2C), private and public schools (B2B), LGUs via Special Education Funds (B2G), and CSR sponsors.

Market sizing (PHP 1,200/learner/yr): 13.5M early learners across PH & SEA (PHP 16.2B/yr TAM), 2.4M Filipino Grade 1 learners (PHP 2.88B/yr SAM), and 185K Region VII learners (PHP 222M/yr SOM; PHP 18M-36M Cebu beachhead), backed by DepEd National Learning Recovery Program.

### Solution Description\* *(292 / 500 words · 1,856 / 2,500 chars)*
PlayIT is a 100% offline-first gamified Android phonics companion guided by Lily the Tarsier, built around the DepEd Marungko sequence across 7 chapters and 26 English letters (starting with M, S, A, I).

Every letter follows a 4-step multisensory loop: (1) Hear It: Lily models the pure phoneme and a Filipino-familiar anchor word (e.g., Mouse) with child-paced replay; (2) Say It: embedded offline Vosk speech recognition checks pronunciation directly on-device at zero cloud cost—if a child misses twice, Lily shows visual mouth-shape articulation cues without deducting hearts; (3) Find It: learners match target sounds to familiar pictures using 5 playful hearts; and (4) Blend It: letter tiles snap into real CVC words (SAM, SIS, AM) with 3-star mastery saved locally in Room SQLite. A Parent Zone translates 26-letter progress into plain language and exports an offline A4 PDF report.

The Android APK requests zero INTERNET permission and is verified by 691 passing automated tests (697 total, 6 skipped by design, 0 failed). In field evaluation across 30 participants (Rounds 1-2: 16 learners, 5 parents, 9 teachers), 15 of 16 learners chose the happiest smileyometer face and 14 of 16 wanted to play again; parents rated Grade 1 fit 4.6/5 (75.5 SUS usability); and teachers scored teaching design 4.73/5 on the final 16-item rubric (n = 5). The evaluation measured engagement, usability, and pedagogy rather than learning gains.

To keep access open while driving sustainability, Chapter 1 (M, S, A, I) stays free forever. Planned launch pricing spans four revenue streams: B2C Family Pass (PHP 149/mo or PHP 999 one-time GCash/ShopeePay voucher), B2B School Licenses (PHP 450/student/yr), B2G LGU Barangay Kits (PHP 75,000 via SEF), and CSR School Adoptions (PHP 150,000), targeting an 88% projected gross margin with PHP 0 cloud inference cost.

### Technology Used\* *(76 / 100 words · 501 / 600 chars)*
Native Android 8.0+ (API 26) mobile app built in Kotlin 1.9.23 with Jetpack Compose, Clean Architecture + MVVM, and Hilt DI. Powered by embedded Vosk 0.3.47 offline automatic speech recognition (on-device small US English model with zero INTERNET permission), Room (SQLite) for local mastery persistence, Media3 ExoPlayer for phoneme/word audio, and iText7 for offline A4 PDF parent report generation. Verified by 697 automated JUnit and Roborazzi UI tests (691 passed, 6 skipped by design, 0 failed).
