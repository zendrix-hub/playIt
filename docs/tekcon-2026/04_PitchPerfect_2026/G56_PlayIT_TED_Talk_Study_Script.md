## ACT I · THE HUMAN STORY (`0:00 – 1:05` | Slides 1–3)
*Delivery tip: Start noticeably **quieter and slower** than a normal presentation. Let the room lean in.*

### `[S01 · 0:00]` — Meet Mia
> *(Stand still. Pause 1 second before your first word.)*
> "Picture a kitchen table in Bohol at seven-thirty tonight. That’s **Mia**. She’s six years old, in Grade 1—staring at **one small letter** under her reading lamp. One sound… she just can’t remember. *(Pause 1s)* Her mother wants so badly to sit beside her—" `[CLICK → S02]`

### `[S02 · 0:20]` — The Curiosity Hook
> *(Gesture lightly upward toward the three bubbles.)*
> "—but look up. Her teacher already has **forty voices** in one room. Nanay doesn't get off work until **nine**. And that phone on the table? **Zero signal.**
> *(Stop for 1.5 seconds. Look straight at the judges.)*
> What happens when nobody at home can answer **one sound**? *(Drop voice slightly)* Mia closes the book." `[CLICK → S03]`

### `[S03 · 0:35]` — Scale the Pain (`9 out of 10`)
> *(Voice becomes firm and clear.)*
> "And Mia is **not** the exception. *(Pause 1s)* According to the June 2022 World Bank and UNESCO Learning Poverty Brief, **90.9 percent**—**nine out of ten** Filipino ten-year-olds—still struggle to read a simple sentence.
> Why haven't global apps fixed this? Two walls: cloud apps freeze without steady Wi-Fi, and four-to-six-hundred-peso monthly paywalls are built for foreign accents." `[CLICK → S04]`

## How to Rehearse in 3 Passes Tonight

1. **Pass 1 (Read the story alone, NO slides):** Practice **Act I (`S01–S03`) + Act IV (`S18`)** twice until you feel the contrast between dim dusk quietness and bright morning warmth.
2. **Pass 2 (Click through on a 5:00 timer):** Run **Presenter View** on the `.pptx`. Notice you do NOT have to rush—every pause (`[PAUSE 1s]`) is built into the `4:52` clock.
3. **Pass 3 (No notes on the story slides):** Stand up, deliver **Slides 1–4 and Slide 18** looking straight across the room without glancing down once. Use Presenter View only for the exact metrics on Slides 10–17!
