P04 · 1:10–2:15 · Solution & Product Demo (Energy lifts — hopeful) · Morph #3
So we built PlayIT—an offline-first Android 8 phonics companion guided by Lily the Tarsier, following DepEd's Marungko order: M, S, A, I first. In four steps per letter: Hear It models /m/; Say It runs offline Vosk speech recognition at zero cloud cost—showing mouth-shape cues on two misses without deducting hearts; Find It matches sounds to pictures; and Blend It snaps tiles into words like SAM in Room SQLite. Parents get plain-language mastery and a one-tap offline A4 PDF—verified by 691 passing tests out of 697 and zero INTERNET permission. [CLICK → Slide 5]
