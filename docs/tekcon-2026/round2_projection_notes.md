# Round 2 projections — where each number comes from

**These are projections, not results.** The survey is still running. They exist so the poster and the script can be laid out now. Before anything is printed or uploaded, replace them in `round2_data.json` with the real figures.

**Design:** 30 participants: **16 Grade 1 learners** (the users, observed while playing), **9 parents** and **5 teachers** (the evaluators). There are no technical evaluators; software quality is covered by the automated tests.

**Basis:**
- The form: `playIT-v2/docs/validation-package/02_GOOGLE_FORM_INSTRUMENT_SPECIFICATION.md`.
- The targets: the SMART table in `01_VALIDATION_FRAMEWORK_AND_SMART_MAPPING.md`. Its group sizes (12, 10, 5, 3) differ from the final design, so update that document.
- The starting point: Round 1 results (25 participants, 16 of them children).
- What changed in the build since Round 1: corrected letter sounds, a stricter speech judge with a mouth-shape cue, the name prompt, the redrawn Lily.

## Things to know before you rely on these

1. **The Round 2 form has no SUS questions**, though the SMART table sets a SUS target of 75. So there is no Round 2 SUS, and the poster shows no SUS figure. Add the ten SUS items to the parent section if you want one; the Round 1 score was 75.5 (n = 5).
2. **Small groups.** With 16 children, one child is about 6 percentage points. Say "12 of 16", not "75%".
3. **This is still feedback on the app, not proof of learning.** Nothing here measures reading improvement.
4. **Keep the raw responses.** Judges can ask to see them.
5. **If any of the 16 children also took part in Round 1,** say so. They already know the app, which can make it look easier than it is for a new child.
6. **Section 5 of the form (technical evaluation) is unused** in this design. Note that in your method section so the form and the report agree.
