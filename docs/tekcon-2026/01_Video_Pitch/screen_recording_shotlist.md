# Screen recordings to capture (real app)

Record on a real phone with the built-in screen recorder, portrait, at the highest quality. Use the newest build, the one with the new Lily and the name prompt. The screenshots in the repo are older than that build, so do not reuse them.

Before you start: turn on Do Not Disturb, clear notifications, set brightness to full, charge above 50% so no battery warning appears.

Save every recording in `assets/recordings/` with the file name shown. A screen recording is cleanest; filming the phone being used also counts as a demonstration of the working system.

**If a child appears or is heard:** get the parent's permission first, and do not show the child's face or full name.
